About

Hello! I am a machine learning research intern at Qlabs. My research focuses on developing trustworthy machine learning in the real world, with a particular interest in the safety and provenance of foundation models. Previously, I graduated summa cum laude from the University of Maryland, College Park with degrees in Computer Science (Honors) and Applied Mathematics.

I am always interested in collaborating! Feel free to send me an email.
